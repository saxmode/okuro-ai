03 Safety guarantees

Five promises you can audit

  • 1

    The model is never load-bearing

    Deterministic fallback on every path
    Evidence ▾

    If inference fails, the rules engine still resolves the request. redteam.py proves the offline path.

  • 2

    PII never leaves the LAN

    Recipient model is firewalled
    Evidence ▾

    Personal data is resolved locally; only shaped claims cross the boundary.

  • 3

    Every claim is grounded

    Entailment re-run after adaptation
    Evidence ▾

    Adaptation is a non-destructive overlay; grounding is re-checked.

  • 4

    Opt-in, not opt-out

    AI is optimisation, not dependency
    Evidence ▾

    Disable AI and the product still works, just slower.

  • 5

    Wrong answers fail loud

    Low confidence flags a human
    Evidence ▾

    Below threshold, the flow escalates rather than guessing.
