How a component is chosen

Every claim is tagged with one of 11 claim-shapes (metric, comparison, quote…). The solver builds a candidate set = components whose fit for that shape is ≥ 0.50, filters by capacity (can it hold the item count?) and emphasis, then ranks by (anti-sameness, −fit, compactness, id) and takes the winner.
