What this is

The board kit as shipped code: structural tokens + hand-authored components + 12 archetype slide templates on a fixed 1600×900 canvas. Three stored brands (resolved by A1 from okuro's brand store) prove the structural/brand split — switch brand top-right; the same components re-theme with zero markup change.

The three brands

okuro — the internal brand: dark, monospace body by design (JetBrains Mono is the brand, not a bug), green accent. A user's own brands appear here once registered; okuro ships none but its own.

Responsiveness model

Slides never reflow — the 1600×900 canvas is transform-scaled to fit any viewport, keeping text vector-crisp and letterboxing in the resolved background. Only the L3 doc-view reflows. Thumbnails are the same slide, scale-transformed (one render path). Resize the window to watch the readout and stages track.
